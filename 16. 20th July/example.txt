# Advantages of Not Extending Built-In Objects:-

1. Avoiding Conflicts:-
By not extending built-in objects, you avoid potential conflicts with other libraries or future changes in the language.

2. Better Readability and Maintenance:-
Utility functions or classes can make your code more readable and easier to maintain.

3. Predictable Behavior:-
Built-in objects behave consistently across different environments and versions of JavaScript when not modified.
